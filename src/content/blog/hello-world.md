This is the first post on my research blog. I plan to use this space to share preliminary ideas, paper summaries, and reflections on the research process.

Writing about research as it happens is different from writing polished papers. It allows for speculation, dead ends, and half-formed thoughts that might otherwise disappear into notebooks.
