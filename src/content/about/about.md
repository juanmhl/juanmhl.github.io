I am a predoctoral researcher at the [Universidad de Málaga (UMA)](https://www.uma.es/), 
where I hold an FPU fellowship in the [Medical Robotics Lab](https://medicalrobotics.uma.es/)
of the [Systems Engineering and Automation Department](https://www.uma.es/isa). My doctoral work focuses on 
human-robot collaboration in minimally invasive surgery, specifically on
the use of machine learning and planning strategies to enable semi-autonomous robotic assistance.


My background is in Mechatronics Engineering (B.Eng. + M.Eng., UMA). 
My thesis research has evolved from Machine Learning approaches to surgical 
gesture recognition toward the use of
Neuro-Symbolic AI techniques for task planning and enhanced perception, 
and learning-based methods for real-time control of robotic manipulators
in surgical contexts.

On the side, I have also contributed to other projects on the use of AI-based
diagnostic tools for Isolated REM Sleep Behaviour Disorder, currently in clinical trials,
and on the development of Digital Twin approaches for several applications.

Beyond research, I have a broad interest in software engineering and open-source tooling, with a side interest in the ethical questions those systems raise, particularly around autonomy and oversight. Outside the lab, I shoot film photography, bake sourdough with inconsistent results, and maintain a caffeine intake that would concern most cardiologists.

I am open to collaborations at the intersection of medical robotics, 
teleoperation, and human-robot interaction. Feel free to reach out.
