I am a Robotics and Mechatronics Engineer and PhD researcher at the [Medical Robotics Laboratory](https://medicalrobotics.uma.es/) of the [Universidad de Málaga (UMA)](https://www.uma.es/), within IBIMA Plataforma BIONAND. I am currently also a visiting researcher at the [Bristol Robotics Laboratory](https://www.bristolroboticslab.com/) at UWE Bristol.

My work focuses on intelligent and collaborative robotic systems, combining machine learning, robot learning, control, and structured reasoning for medical and surgical robotics. I have worked on surgical activity recognition, neuro-symbolic reasoning, learning from demonstration, trajectory generation, teleoperation, and force/motion control, with hands-on experience using Python, PyTorch, ROS, MATLAB, real robotic platforms, and haptic devices.

Through medical robotics projects, I have also gained experience with medical-device regulatory processes, technical documentation, experimental validation, and clinical study protocols.

Beyond research, I have a broad interest in software engineering and open-source tooling. Outside the lab, I shoot film photography, bake sourdough with inconsistent results, and maintain a caffeine intake that would concern most cardiologists.

I am particularly interested in Robotics R&D, autonomous systems, human-robot interaction, and applied machine learning. Feel free to reach out for collaborations, technical discussions, or industry opportunities.
