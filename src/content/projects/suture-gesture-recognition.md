Recognition of laparoscopic suturing surgical gestures from kinematic data using feature selection and both Hidden Markov Models and Neural Networks, validated on the JIGSAWS benchmark. [Published in IEEE Access](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3516949). Repo [here](https://github.com/juanmhl/uma-lapsuture-gestures-recognition).

Matlab · ROS · JIGSAWS dataset
