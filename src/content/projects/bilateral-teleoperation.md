order: 4

A ROS 2 teleoperation system coupling a 3D Systems Touch haptic device to a UR3e robotic arm, designed for surgical data collection. Provides synchronized kinematic and force/torque streams at 500 Hz, enabling the acquisition of expert demonstration datasets for learning-based surgical assistance research. Repo under development.

C++ · Python · ROS 2 · Docker
