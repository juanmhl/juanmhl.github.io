title: "Sutura automatizada para una plataforma robótica de asistencia a la cirugía laparoscópica"

venue: "Revista Iberoamericana de Automática e Informática Industrial (RIAI)"
authors: "Galán-Cuenca Á, De Luis-Moura D, Herrera-López JM, Rollón M, García-Morales I, Muñoz VF"

---
Presents a vision-guided and force-position controlled automated suturing algorithm, validated on a two-arm platform — one arm operating through the fulcrum constraint and one intra-abdominal camera arm — as a first step towards autonomous robotic suturing in laparoscopic surgery.
