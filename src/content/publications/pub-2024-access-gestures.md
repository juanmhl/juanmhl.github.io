Applies the ReliefF algorithm to the JIGSAWS kinematic dataset to identify the most informative variables for laparoscopic suture gesture recognition, showing that tool aperture angle and orientation alone preserve classification accuracy across MLP and HMM models — with direct implications for sensor selection in deployment platforms.
