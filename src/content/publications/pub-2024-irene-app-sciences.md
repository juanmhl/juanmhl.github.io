Extends the public ROSMA surgical dataset with two annotated subsets — bounding boxes for instrument detection (ROSMAT24) and multi-level gesture labels (ROSMAG40) — and validates them using YOLOv4 and a bidirectional LSTM, providing new benchmarks for surgical task recognition research.
