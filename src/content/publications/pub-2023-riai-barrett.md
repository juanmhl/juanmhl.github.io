title: "Entorno de trabajo cíber-físico para cirugía laparoscópica"
year: 2023
venue: "Revista Iberoamericana de Automática e Informática Industrial (RIAI)"
authors: "Herrera-López JM, Galán-Cuenca Á, García-Morales I, Rollón M, Rivas-Blanco I, Muñoz VF"
url: "https://doi.org/10.4995/riai.2023.18753"
---
Develops a cyber-physical framework that integrates a laparoscopic surgical simulator with a redundant robotic arm under ROS and MATLAB, proposing and validating workspace configurations and an inverse kinematics model that guarantees compliance with the fulcrum constraint.
